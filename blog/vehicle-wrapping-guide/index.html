Vehicle Wrapping 101: Turn Your Fleet Into Moving Billboards

Apr 15, 2026

Advertising that moves

A single wrapped vehicle can generate tens of thousands of brand impressions per day as it travels the city — at a fraction of the cost-per-impression of most other media.

Full vs partial wraps

  • Full wrap: maximum impact, covers the whole vehicle
  • Partial wrap: doors, panels or rear — budget-friendly
  • Lettering & decals: simple contact details and logo

How the process works

We design to your vehicle's exact template, print on premium cast vinyl, laminate for protection, then professionally install — smoothing every curve and recess for a paint-like finish.

A quality cast-vinyl wrap also protects your original paint and can be removed cleanly later.

Caring for your wrap

  • Hand wash; avoid harsh brushes
  • Park in shade when possible
  • Remove spills promptly
  • Expect 5+ years of life with good care
