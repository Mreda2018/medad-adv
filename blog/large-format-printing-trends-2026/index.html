Large-Format Printing Trends to Watch in 2026

Mar 10, 2026

1. Texture and dimension

Flat is out. UV printing now lets brands add raised textures, spot gloss and tactile 3D detail that make signage and packaging feel premium and worth touching.

2. Sustainable substrates

Recyclable boards, solvent-free inks and reusable stand hardware are moving from nice-to-have to expected, especially for events and retail.

3. Personalisation at scale

DTF and UV technology make short, personalised runs affordable — so brands can localise campaigns, print variable data and produce on demand without big minimums.

4. Bolder color and contrast

With wider color gamuts and white-ink layering, designs are getting brighter and more confident — built to stop the scroll in the physical world.

Want help applying these trends to your next campaign? Medad's team can advise on materials, finishes and budget.

5. Integrated digital touchpoints

QR codes, NFC tags and AR triggers printed onto signage bridge the gap between physical displays and online journeys — turning a banner into a measurable channel.
