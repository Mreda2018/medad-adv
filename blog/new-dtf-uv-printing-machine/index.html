Inside Our New DTF & UV Printing Machine: What It Means for Your Brand

May 28, 2026

A leap in printing capability

At Medad Advertising we never stop upgrading. Our newest investment is a combined DTF (Direct-to-Film) and UV printing machine — a single platform that dramatically widens what we can print, the surfaces we can print on, and the durability of the final result.

For our clients in Dubai and across the UAE, this means faster turnaround, richer color, and the ability to brand products and materials that older printing methods simply couldn't handle.

What is DTF printing?

DTF (Direct-to-Film) transfers a design printed on a special film onto fabric and other surfaces using heat and adhesive powder. The standout advantage is a crisp, full-color print with a soft hand-feel that stretches and flexes without cracking.

  • Vibrant, photographic detail and gradients
  • White ink for printing on dark or transparent items
  • Excellent wash and stretch durability
  • No minimum quantity — ideal for one-offs and small runs

What is UV printing?

UV printing cures ink instantly with ultraviolet light the moment it hits the surface. Because the ink dries on contact rather than soaking in, it sits crisply on rigid and unusual materials — and can even be layered for a raised, textured 3D effect.

  • Prints directly on acrylic, wood, glass, metal, leather and more
  • Scratch- and water-resistant finish
  • Raised/embossed textures and spot-gloss effects
  • Sharp small text and fine lines

What we can now produce for you

Combining both technologies under one roof means Medad can deliver branded products and signage that used to require multiple suppliers:

  • Custom gifts, awards and promotional items
  • Personalised packaging and labels
  • Acrylic signage and reception logos with 3D texture
  • Branded apparel, bags and sports kits
  • Short-run, on-demand personalisation

Have a product or surface in mind? Send us a photo and dimensions — we'll tell you exactly how DTF or UV printing can bring it to life.

Why it matters

Modern brands need to move quickly and produce in small, flexible batches without sacrificing quality. Our DTF & UV machine lets us say yes to ideas that were previously too costly or too slow — and to deliver them with a premium, durable finish that protects your brand image.
